CASE STUDY 01

Apex Logistics APAC: partner onboarding cut from 42.5 to 24.0 days

Scope
Supply chain ERP and API integration
Delivery period
June to November 2024 (6 months)
Client
Cross-border distribution across Singapore, Malaysia and Indonesia

The problem

Partner onboarding relied on manual document checks across separate legacy databases in Singapore and Johor. A baseline audit on 28 May 2024 measured an average of 42.5 business days from partner document submission to ERP profile activation, taken from internal timestamp logs.

What we built

  1. Discovery and schema mapping1 Jun to 15 Jul 2024

    We mapped the legacy database structures and designed unified REST endpoints connecting SAP inventory nodes to regional partner portals.

  2. Portal and automated verification16 Jul to 30 Sep 2024

    We built a secure vendor-facing ordering portal with OCR-based trade licence verification to reduce data-entry errors.

  3. Staged rollout1 Oct to 30 Nov 2024

    We deployed across 34 active regional distributors.

Measured results

1,420Orders per monthAcross 34 active distributorsSource: automated ERP order logs
99.1%Data accuracySource: automated multicurrency cross-check

Cycle time is measured the same way before and after: from initial partner document submission to final ERP profile activation, using internal timestamp logs.

CASE STUDY 02

OmniTrade Enterprise: order processing cut from 36 hours to 12.5 minutes

Scope
B2B wholesale ordering portal and ERP integration
Delivery period
September 2024 to January 2025 (5 months)
Client
Industrial hardware and raw materials supplier serving plants across Southeast Asia

The problem

OmniTrade handled over 800 bulk orders a month by phone and emailed attachments, causing stockouts and order discrepancies. A baseline analysis on 20 August 2024 recorded 36 hours from quote request to warehouse pick ticket, and a 7.4% error rate in manual SKU transcription.

What we built

  1. Architecture and ERP integration1 Sep to 15 Oct 2024

    We built bi-directional API middleware connecting the on-premise inventory database to a new customer ordering portal.

  2. Portal and tiered pricing16 Oct to 10 Dec 2024

    We built role-based ordering with volume pricing tiers, credit-limit verification and automated tax calculation for Singapore and regional customs.

  3. Pilot launch and audit11 Dec 2024 to 31 Jan 2025

    We onboarded top-tier industrial clients. The system audit on 28 Jan 2025 confirmed processing under 15 minutes.

Measured results

12.5 minQuote request to pick ticketBaseline 36 hours (20 Aug 2024)Source: system audit logs, 28 Jan 2025
0.3%SKU error rateBaseline 7.4% manual transcription errorsSource: pilot audit, Jan 2025
$630kOrder value processedIn the 7-week pilot windowSource: portal order records

Results cover the pilot with top-tier clients, not the client’s full order volume. Baselines were recorded before work began.

“The B2B ordering portal engineered by GlowSoft drastically reduced manual order entry errors and gave our wholesale clients reliable stock visibility.”

Elena Lim, Managing Director, OmniTrade Enterprise
